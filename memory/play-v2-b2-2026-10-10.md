## The lazy-image mistake worth keeping

First build: every card stayed a chip and Chromium made **no** request to Scryfall at all.
The `<img>` was `display: none` until loaded, and a `loading="lazy"` image with no box is
never "in the viewport", so the browser never fetches it. Fix: keep it in layout at
`opacity: 0` until `onload`. The gate pins `loading="lazy"` + `onerror` per `<img>`.

## Activation labels — why the matcher refuses

The layer-resolved `ActivatedAbility.description` is empty by construction
(`replay_harness` lowering), so the only prose is `Characteristics.rules_text` (= the
def's `oracle_text`). The cost is the join: render the `ActivationCost` prefix (mana,
`{T}`, "Pay N life") and take the oracle line starting with it; keyword-cost lines
("Equip {1}") match without a colon; `{T}: Add …` lines are excluded (mana abilities are
not in `activated_abilities`). The matcher returns `None` unless the candidate count
equals the number of abilities sharing the prefix, because a wrong line on a button is
worse than the index form. `calculate_characteristics` is not a gated `GameState` read, so
`test_ui6_view_rs_reads_game_state_in_exactly_the_three_known_places` is untouched.

## Seed 5 found three small dead ends, all closed here

1. **A lit eligible attacker said "No legal action".** `offeredIds` lights the creatures in
   `DeclareAttackers.attack.eligible`, but `handleCardClick` only matched actions by
   `object_id`, which a declare action does not have. Now a click on an eligible attacker or
   blocker opens the declare picker.
2. **Auto-open counted unanswerable offers.** Red Elemental Blast with zero candidates in
   both slots was a "real" action, so the lone declare did not open. `real` now filters by
   `answerable`.
3. **"⏭ to my next turn" / "⏵ to next step" on a declare decision** were clicks that did
   nothing (`startPassUntil` stops at once on a non-`Priority` decision). Hidden there; the
   pass button reads "Attack with nothing" / "Block with nothing" on a declare decision,
   since that is what passing is.

What the driver was actually stuck on is **`OOS-DX20b-1`** (already in the registry): seed 5,
turn 9, Purphoros, God of the Forge with devotion 1 is offered as the one eligible attacker
and the engine refuses the declaration ("not a creature"). The surface shows the refusal in
the error strip and Back works; the greedy driver now backs out of a refused offer and
passes. Not a v2 defect; not fixed here.
