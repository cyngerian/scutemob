## Decisions and departures

- **Root font 13px on the v3 page** (the mockup's), so `PromptPanel` and the pickers (designed at
  16px) are scaled back with `zoom: 1.23` in `v3-dock.css`.
- **Grey-out lift** (brief-only rule, not in the mockup): lifted while a picker chain is open or the
  decision is `DeclareBlockers`. Hover follows the mockup (opacity .7), which wins over the brief.
- **"▲ across" tag**: the mockup's rule targets a `.hdr` its own cell never renders, so the tag was
  invisible there; here it sits under the life total.
- **Lands are real stacks** (`landStacks`) for every seat; the mockup faked opponents' as two cards.
- **Focused board is sized for the largest opponent** (as the mockup), so a focus change never resizes.
- **Decision box**: re-opens on a new `seq`, on pregame / game over, and when a picker opens; never
  floats during an auto-pass run (it would strobe). Card items only on `Priority`; anything whose
  `object_id` is not a visible card stays a `PromptPanel` button (`renderedIds` = card-index keys).
- **Exile** is the one shared list in every box, labelled `EX*` with a note (per-player is C3).
- **No seed in the top bar**: `GameSummary` deliberately does not carry it (MR-M11-01).
- **Popovers, the seat menu and the peeked cell render at the table root** (`ui.svelte.js`): the
  greyed cell's `filter` would otherwise become the containing block of a fixed popover.

## Deferred

- **C2 (`-267`)**: arrow targeting, card context menus, blocker arrows (the mockup's `blockUi`),
  the auto-pass panel (C1 has `PromptPanel`'s two pass-until buttons), summoning-sick outline,
  drag-to-rearrange. `CardFace` already carries `data-oid` / `data-name`, cells `data-seat`.
- **C3 (`-268`)**: per-player exile, commander tax on the CMD pile, revealed cards, image proxy
  (the rate-limit rows above), pass-until-step, `verify.py` on v3, game to game-over.
- No log panel (the mockup's `log` button does nothing either); `targeted` red outline is a
  `CardFace` prop nothing sets yet (the seat view has no targeting flag on a permanent).

## LOW / known

- A creature carrying loyalty counters would overlap its two P/T boxes (mockup has no rule either).
- Command-zone cards show no ★ (`CardInZoneView` has no `is_commander`).
- Name bands hyphenate oddly mid-word at small sizes (mockup CSS, verbatim).
- The greedy driver never blocks and never uses "Fail to find"; the blocker picker was opened and
  confirmed empty, not exercised with a real block.
