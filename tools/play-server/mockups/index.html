Play surface v2 — five layout candidates

Static, same sample state in all five: turn 10, Bot-3's main phase, Beast Within on the stack targeting your Thalia, you hold priority with two instants lit. Open each full-screen at your normal zoom. Nothing is wired.

6 · Table (owner's layout)

You in the middle: creatures / other / smaller lands rows, hand under. Opponents' compact boards in the side columns; click one to bring them across the table at the top; a turn change does it too. Right-click a seat for graveyard / exile. Every card magnifies on hover and feeds the card window ↗ (pop it to the second monitor). Dock on the right: phase dial, stack, prompt.

+ mockup-only controls in the header: state switcher (4 board states incl. late game), card-size slider, "next turn"

− designed at 2560×1340 at 100 % zoom; checked at 1707×880

1 · Table

Classic client shape. Thin opponent strips on top, your battlefield as the big middle, a one-line prompt bar, hand bar, log drawer on the right.

+ familiar; battlefield ≈ 60% of height; everything has a home

− still boxes-and-bands; log column spends 280px

2 · Arena

One playmat, no boxes. Opponent lanes mirrored across the top, your creatures front and centre, rocks and lands in a low row, fanned hand, life avatars at the left, one big action button bottom-right.

+ feels like a game; the creature row is huge; the action is one button

− opponent boards compressed to lanes; log hidden

3 · Pod

A four-seat table. Opponents sit left, top and right around a centre table that holds the stack and the prompt; your board is the wide bottom band.

+ every board visible in its seat; the stack is literally on the table

− side seats are narrow; your board gets the least height of the five

4 · Focus

Your board is the whole screen. Opponents are seat tiles in a left rail you click to swap the main area to their board. Prompt sentence pinned on top. Log is a bottom sheet.

+ biggest cards of the five; one sentence always tells you what to do

− opponents' boards are a click away, not glanceable

5 · Columns

Every seat is a vertical column, yours double width. Each column is a full board top to bottom. Prompt and stack in one band on top, hand on the bottom.

+ the whole table at once, nothing hidden, scales to 3–6 seats

− columns wrap cards early; your column is tall but narrow
